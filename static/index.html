Who We Are

About Elevora

Elevora is an intelligent Career Acceleration and Resume Intelligence platform that bridges the gap between candidate qualifications and recruiter hiring standards using advanced Data Science, NLP, and Explainable AI.

Our Mission

To eliminate ambiguity in the modern hiring process. We empower candidates with transparent, data-driven insights into how their skills align with enterprise job benchmarks.

Multi-Domain Intelligence

Powered by TF-IDF vector space modeling, 250+ skill taxonomy covering Full Stack, AI, Cloud, DevOps, Mobile, and Cybersecurity, with multi-dimensional match scoring.

Career Acceleration
